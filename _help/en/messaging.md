League2GO gives managers two ways to communicate: **messages** you send to a chosen group of players, and an **in-app chat** for ongoing conversation. This guide covers both.

## Sending a message

To send a message, go to the **Messaging** tab of your league. Write your message, select the recipients, and send. No mailing list or individual texting needed. You can address:

* **The whole league**: for example a rule change or an end-of-season announcement.
* **A team**: information relevant to one roster only.
* **The players of a game**: ideal for a venue change or a cancellation notice for that game.

## How players receive messages

Players are reached according to their own [notification preferences](/notifications/): email, push notification or SMS.

* **Email** includes the complete content of the message.
* **Push notifications and SMS** carry the title only, with a link to read the full message.
* When a player signs in with an unread message waiting, a notification badge appears on their Messages menu, where they can read their recent messages.

If a player says they didn't get your message, have them check their notification preferences and spam folder. See [notifications and reminders](/notifications/) and [mobile notifications](/mobile-notifications/).

Besides one-way messages, League2GO offers a chat where players can talk with each other in the app.
