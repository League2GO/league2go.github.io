This guide walks you through setting up a league on League2GO, from creating your account to publishing your first games. Each step links to a detailed article if you need more depth.

## 1. Create your account

Go to [league2go.com](https://league2go.com/?lang=en#/signup) and enter the requested information. One account is enough to be a manager or a player in several leagues. See [your account](/your-account/) for how accounts and logins work.

## 2. Create your league

Leagues are created **on the website** at [league2go.com](https://league2go.com), not in the mobile app.

From the Leagues menu, click Create a league and enter the requested information:

* **Sport** — defines the available position configurations and the statistics that can be calculated after each game.
* **League type** — fixed teams or pick-up. Choose carefully: the type cannot be changed after the league is created. See [league types](/league-types/).
* **Positions** — used by League2GO to automatically replace an absent player with a substitute who plays the same position.

{% include screenshot.html description="Leagues menu > Create a league form, showing the sport, league type and positions fields, with the league type selector highlighted" %}

Your first games are free, then you buy game credits as you go. See [pricing and game credits](/pricing-and-game-credits/).

## 3. Add your teams

Two teams are automatically added to your league. To rename a team or change its color and logo, click its gear icon. If your league has more than two teams, use the Add a team button.

{% include screenshot.html description="League page > Teams tab, showing two team sections with their gear icons and the Add a team button highlighted" %}

For captains, team colors, logos and other options, see [teams and team settings](/teams-and-team-settings/).

## 4. Add your players

To add a player, click the + icon at the bottom of a team section. Enter the player's email address or cell number: the player uses **exactly** this information to sign in to League2GO, so double-check it. Once added, the player receives an automated message inviting them to complete their registration, which gives them access to the league schedule.

{% include screenshot.html description="Teams tab > Add a player dialog, showing the email and cell number fields with a note that the player signs in with these exact coordinates" %}

There are no invite links — matching contact information is what connects a player to your league. See [adding players](/adding-players/) for the details, and [managing players](/managing-players/) for what you can do afterwards.

## 5. Add your substitutes

In the Substitutes tab, add substitutes for a specific team, or league substitutes shared across all teams. Substitutes receive an invitation to register, then automatic requests when a player of their position declares an absence. Check every position a substitute can play so they are contacted as often as possible.

{% include screenshot.html description="League page > Substitutes tab, showing the per-team substitute sections and the league substitutes section, with a + add icon highlighted" %}

See [managing substitutes](/managing-substitutes/) and, to understand how the automatic search works, the [substitute search overview](/substitutes-overview/).

## 6. Create your schedule

From the league calendar, click Add and enter the game information. If your games are weekly, use the repeat option to create multiple games with the same parameters in one step. Games are created and managed **on the website**.

{% include screenshot.html description="League calendar on the website, showing a month of scheduled games and the Add game button highlighted" %}

See [schedule and calendar](/schedule-and-calendar/) for repeating games, cancellations and the season filter.

## 7. Review your league settings (optional)

In the Settings tab you can add other [managers](/roles-and-access/), create [seasons](/seasons/), configure your [statistics](/results-standings-statistics/), tune the [substitute search](/substitute-search-settings/) and adjust automated [notifications](/notifications/). You can also set up a public [league website](/league-website/).

Your players can use the free [mobile app](/mobile-app/) to see their schedule and declare absences.
