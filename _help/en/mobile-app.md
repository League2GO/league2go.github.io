The League2GO mobile app gives you access to your leagues anytime, from your iPhone or Android phone. It is **free for everyone**: managers, players and substitutes.

The app is optional: everything players need is also available on the website at [league2go.com](https://league2go.com). We still recommend it, because once installed it delivers **push notifications** for game reminders, substitution requests and messages. See [mobile notifications](/mobile-notifications/).

## What you can do in the app

**Players:**

* View your schedule and the lineup of each game
* Declare an absence in seconds
* Sync your games to your phone's calendar (see [calendar sync](/mobile-calendar-sync/))
* View standings, statistics and game results

**Substitutes:**

* Open a substitution request with one tap and accept it in an instant

**Managers:**

* Follow absences and substitutions as they happen
* Manage lineups and league settings
* Send messages to players and substitutes
* Enter game results and player statistics
