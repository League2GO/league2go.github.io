This guide describes how the League2GO app **syncs your player schedule to your calendar app** on your phone, and provides helpful
**troubleshooting tips** to help you resolve common issues.

## How it works

Once you activate calendar sync,
the League2GO app will add your upcoming games to your calendar app on your phone.

* For iOS, games are added to the **iOS Calendar app**.
* For Android, games are added to the **Google Calendar app**.

You can always open the League2GO app to initiate a sync.

* **Always use the app** to declare your absence or presence.
* Always use the app if you **think a game might be cancelled**.
