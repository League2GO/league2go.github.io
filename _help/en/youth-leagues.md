A youth league is a league whose players are under 18. The children do not hold accounts; their parents do. League2GO writes to the parents, the parents answer for their child, and a youth league never spends a game credit.

## Choosing the youth audience

**Audience** is one of the fields you fill in when you [create your league](/create-your-league/), next to the sport and the league type. Set it to **Youth** and the league changes shape:

* Players are entered without an email address or a cell number of their own.
* Each player carries a list of **parents**, and it is the parents who get accounts.
* Scheduling a game costs no [game credit](/pricing-and-game-credits/).

You choose the audience when the league is created. The league type (fixed teams or pick-up) and the sport are separate choices; see [league types](/league-types/).

## Adding a child and their parents

Open the **Teams** tab and click **Add player** below the team's roster, the same way as in any league (see [adding players](/adding-players/)). In a youth league the form asks for the child's name, position and jersey number, and then for their **parents**.

Add a parent with their first name, last name, and an email address or a cell number. Add as many parents as the family needs: a child can carry two parents, or more. You can also leave a note on a parent, which shows next to their name on the roster.

Each parent is invited to register with the exact email address or cell number you entered, exactly as a player would be in an adult league. The **child is never invited and never registers**: they have no account.

On the roster, the contact column shows each parent's name, email address and phone number, and marks any parent who has not finished registering yet.

## What parents receive

In a youth league, the messages that would go to the player go to **every parent of that child instead**. Each parent gets their own copy, through their own notification settings.

Parents are notified when:

* a game is added to the calendar;
* a game reminder goes out before the game;
* the lineup for a game is published;
* a substitute is requested for their child's spot, and what it costs;
* a league manager sends a message to the team or to the league.

For these, each parent chooses email, push notifications, or both, in their own profile. See [notifications and reminders](/notifications/) and [mobile notifications](/mobile-notifications/).

## What parents can do

A parent signs in with their own account. Their schedule lists every game of every one of their children, and each game carries the name of the child it belongs to. A parent with two children in the club sees both children's games in one list.

From a game, a parent can:

* confirm their child will play, or declare an absence;
* accept or refuse an open spot offered to their child;
* pay the league online for their child, when the league collects payments (see [paying online](/player-payments-guide/)).

There is no separate parent site: parents use the same schedule, game and profile screens as everyone else, with their child's name shown on the games that belong to them.

## What a youth league costs

Nothing. An adult league buys game credits and spends one for each scheduled game; a youth league spends none, no matter how many games the season holds. Nobody in the league pays League2GO: not the children, not the parents, not the coaches, not the managers. See [pricing and game credits](/pricing-and-game-credits/) for how credits work in adult leagues.
