Seasons group your league's games, standings, and statistics. Each season has a start and end date. Standings and statistics open on the current season, so they start fresh each year. The schedule opens on **All seasons**; pick a season and it only shows the games inside that season's dates.

## "My games disappeared"

If you picked a season and games are missing from your schedule, the season filter is the reason: those games fall outside that season's dates. Nothing is deleted; the games are only hidden.

To see them again, do one of the following:

* **Show every season**: click **Show all seasons** above the list, or the **X** beside the season name. The schedule goes back to **All seasons**.
* **Extend the season dates**: in your league settings, edit the season so its start and end dates cover the games' dates.

{% include image.html src="help/seasons-schedule-filter.en.png" caption="The league schedule with a season picked in the Season filter; Show all seasons, or the X beside the season name, clears the filter and shows every season's games." %}

## Creating a season

To create a season, go to **Settings** > **Season list**. Add a season with a name and its start and end dates. All games whose date falls within that range belong to the season.

{% include image.html src="help/seasons-settings.en.png" caption="Settings > Season list: every season is listed with its start and end dates, and the Add season button creates the next one." %}

Make sure the date range actually covers all the games you plan to schedule. See ["My games disappeared"](#my-games-disappeared) above.

## Starting a new season each year

Create a new season in your league settings at the start of each year (or session). Standings and statistics are calculated per season, so the new season starts fresh at zero.

Previous seasons are never lost: the old seasons and their standings and statistics are kept, and you can view them at any time by selecting a past season from the drop-down menu.

For more on how results feed standings and statistics, see [results, standings and statistics](/results-standings-statistics/#standings-and-tiebreakers).

## Playoffs

To keep playoff standings and statistics separate from the regular season, create the playoffs as their own season. Give it a date range that does not overlap the regular season, so each game belongs to exactly one season.
