League2GO is the **app your league uses to manage itself** — it is not a league. Your league is run by its own manager, and each question has its right door.

## Contact your league manager for…

* **Joining or leaving a league** — managers add and remove players; League2GO cannot sign you up for a league.
* **Getting on (or off) the substitute list.**
* **Game locations, times, and schedule changes.**
* **League rules and fees** — how much you pay and what for.
* **Refunds** — payments go to your league's own Stripe account, so only your manager can refund you. See [paying online](/player-payments-guide/).

Your manager is the person who added you to the league — typically listed on your league's page.

## Contact League2GO support for…

* **Login and account problems** — password resets that fail, duplicate accounts, "email already in use". See [your account](/your-account/).
* **App bugs** — something broken or behaving strangely.
* **Notifications not working** — after trying the steps in [notifications and reminders](/notifications/).
* **Questions about how the app works.**

When in doubt: if it's about **your league** (its games, rules, money, or roster), ask your manager; if it's about **the app itself**, ask us.

Questions? Contact us at [info@league2go.com](mailto:info@league2go.com).
