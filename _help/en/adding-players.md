This guide explains how to add players to your league, how they get access to their schedule, and how to make their onboarding smooth.

## How joining works: there is no invite link

Understanding this model is the key to a smooth start for every player:

* When you add a player, you enter their **email address and cell number**. League2GO automatically sends them a welcome message inviting them to register; you never need to create or share an invite link.
* The player then creates their own account at [league2go.com](https://league2go.com) using the **exact same email address and cell number** you entered. This match is what links their account to your league.
* One account is enough for everything: a person uses the same League2GO account to be a player, substitute, captain or manager in any number of leagues.

If the player signs up with a different email or phone number than the one you typed, their account will not be linked to your league and they will not see the schedule.

## Adding a player to a team

To add a player, open the **Teams** tab of your league and click the **Add player** button below the team's roster. Enter the player's email address or cell number: this is the information they will use to sign in to League2GO. You can also set their positions, jersey number and other details (see [managing players](/managing-players/)).

## Automatic registration reminders

Players often lose the welcome message. League2GO automatically sends a reminder to players who have not completed their registration after a week, so you don't have to chase them. Until they register, they can't declare their own absences; you or the team captain must do it for them.

Note that SMS invitations stop being sent to players who still haven't registered after about a month. If that happens, the fastest fix is usually to delete and re-add the player, or to ask them to register directly at [league2go.com](https://league2go.com) with the same email and cell number.

## Fixing a typo in an email or phone number

* **Before the player has registered**: delete the player from the team and add them again with the correct email address and cell number. A new invitation is sent automatically.
* **After the player has registered**: only the player can edit their own contact information, from their own [account](/your-account/). Managers cannot change a registered player's email or phone number.

If a player says "I can't see the league", compare letter for letter the email and cell number in your league with the ones on their account: a mismatch is almost always the cause.

Once your players are in, see [managing players](/managing-players/) to move them between teams, convert them to substitutes, and fill in their jersey numbers, positions and skills.
